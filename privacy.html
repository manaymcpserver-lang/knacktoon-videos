Privacy policy

Knacktoon Uploader · last updated 4 November 2026

Knacktoon Uploader is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Knacktoon YouTube channels to upload and schedule that owner's own videos.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google sign-in, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish times, and read basic details of the owner's own channels and videos. It does not access anyone else's data.

What it stores

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the owner's computer, so the tool can upload without signing in each time. No data is collected from viewers, and nothing is sold, shared or sent to any third party.

Google's policies

Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Knacktoon Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the owner then deletes the stored token.
